# Portable operating rules

Ballard Trading Co. is a collecting business built to support the hobby. Cards and comics are the content. Keep the primary brand simple: lowercase ballard, clear typography, restrained graphics and authentic product photos. Do not invent athlete likenesses or substitute generated images for item originals. Social imagery and marketplace publication require the owner's applicable explicit approval.

## Listing workflow

- Core is the hub. Show ready cards, put only exceptions and their correction fields at the front of one scrolling page, save valid corrections independently and preserve rejected edits.
- Keep exact master SKUs, original image bytes, storage locations and per-channel prices. Existing CDP IDs remain valid identities even after CDP is retired.
- Use already identified card facts. Do not ask for the year again if the identification supplies it. Owner direction defaults Hoody's cards or cards less than three years old to Mint, and others to Good, with editable owner review. A default is not independent grading.
- Whole-dollar eBay asking prices; preserve independent CollX pricing. Cheap cards may use one original photo. Better cards can require additional evidence without stopping unrelated ready cards.
- Use the single existing payment arrangement automatically. Keep actual shipping measurements and valid marketplace policy requirements visible when they need correction.
- Preview the exact listing content and original photos, then one final Publish confirmation. A cancellation makes no approval/publication call. Reconcile unknown outcomes before retrying.
- For comics, preserve the existing condition/disclosure and offer rules in BTConnect; do not apply card condition defaults to comics.

## Owner card packing rules — October 23, 2026 Pacific

- Every ungraded single card under $20 uses a plain white envelope on eBay and CollX. Do not ask for its shipping method per card.
- PWE setup: #10 envelope, sleeve, top loader and paper wrap; typically under 2 oz and under 0.25 in thick. Form allowances are 2 oz and 0.24 in, with the standard 9.5 × 4.125 in footprint. These are owner-provided bounds and working estimates, not independently measured values.
- Cards over $20 use a 4.75 × 8.75 in mailer, approximately 1 in thick, at 3 oz. Retain graded-card and marketplace eligibility requirements.
- Packaging settings do not approve listing content or publication; show the exact completed review once.

## Inventory and sales

Confirmed marketplace order evidence, exact item mapping and quantity are required before changing stock. Missing listings, stale CSVs or matching titles are not proof of a sale. Replay each order line safely once. Refunds do not automatically restock an item. When cross-channel removal cannot be verified, show the item, channel, listing link and reason in a manual-action queue.

## Buying and economics

Prioritize sustainable turnover and positive net dollars. Include acquisition, shipping, supplies, marketplace fees and at least 5% eBay promotion in flip estimates. Label asking prices separately from sold evidence. Preserve purchase authorization boundaries; development work or research is not buying authority.

## Documentation

Keep general rules here and in executable tests. Store private customer, financial and inventory records only in the private operating system/backups. Record changed owner decisions with date and implementation evidence; avoid leaving essential rules only in a conversation.
