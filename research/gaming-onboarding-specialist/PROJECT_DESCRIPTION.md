# Gaming Onboarding Specialist — Project Description

Gaming Onboarding Specialist is a comprehensive, continuously updated U.S. online gaming regulatory intelligence platform designed to provide expert-level legal research and actionable guidance for gaming merchant onboarding, payment processing, and compliance.

The goal is to develop a searchable knowledge base that delivers deeper, more accurate, and more current regulatory insights than traditional internal reference materials.

The platform covers all 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C., with additional coverage of federal regulations and applicable tribal gaming jurisdictions.

## Gaming categories
- Real-money online sports betting
- Online casinos (iGaming)
- Online poker
- Daily fantasy sports (DFS)
- Pick'em fantasy contests
- Sweepstakes casinos and promotional gaming
- Advance-deposit wagering (ADW) and horse racing
- Prediction markets and event contracts
- Online lotteries and lottery couriers
- Online bingo and charitable gaming
- Skill-based gaming

## Core capabilities
1. **State-by-state regulatory intelligence:** Track legality, licensing requirements, restrictions, exemptions, and enforcement activity by gaming category.
2. **Continuous legal monitoring:** Identify changes to statutes, regulations, legislation, court decisions, and regulatory guidance.
3. **Payments compliance expertise:** Analyze obligations and exposure for payment processors, payment facilitators, sponsor banks, gaming operators, and vendors.
4. **Natural-language research:** Answer complex questions with precise legal citations, effective dates, jurisdiction-specific analysis, and confidence levels.
5. **Merchant onboarding guidance:** Translate regulatory requirements into actionable onboarding considerations, documentation requirements, risk flags, and legal escalation recommendations.
6. **Historical change tracking:** Maintain versioned regulatory developments and distinguish proposed, enacted, effective, disputed, and superseded requirements.

## Research standards
Prioritize official government, regulator, legislative, and court sources. Every substantive legal conclusion must be supported by verifiable evidence. Unknown or disputed legal status must never be represented as permission to operate.

## Ultimate objective
Build a trusted, continuously improving gaming regulatory research assistant that identifies regulatory risks, challenges outdated assumptions, and provides comprehensive, source-backed answers to complex gaming and payments questions. It is a research and decision-support resource, not an independent legal approval authority.
