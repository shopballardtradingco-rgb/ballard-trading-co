# BTCore 0.3.5 — back photos optional for every card

At the owner’s direction, all trading cards may be approved, exported and received with only a front photo, regardless of price, autograph, numbering, grade, classification or legacy two-sided photo policy. No cutoff or override is needed. Existing saved backs remain intact and continue appearing in exports. Unpictured reverse condition is not represented as inspected.

The shared browser/server rule applies immediately to existing drafts without modifying records or approvals. Card cutoff controls are removed from intake, and review guidance makes optional backs explicit. Identity, price, condition and front-photo requirements remain in place. Comic and other-collectible photo rules are unchanged.

Validation covers expensive front-only cards, legacy policy values, approval/export, receiving and a mixed mobile completion flow with an optional back upload.
