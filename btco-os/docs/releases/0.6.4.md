# BTCore 0.6.4 — Inline listing edits and shared PWE shipping

## Authorized scope

Tim requested condition dropdowns and price/condition/shipping edits directly on card rows, shared PWE shipping for singles through $20, and more careful comic/lot shipping. Standing authorization covers tested development deployment, not publishing real listings or changing existing eBay policies.

## Changes

- Inline controls on Intake, card review, saved unpublished batches and linked Vault rows. Explicit row/save-all actions retain other unsaved rows across navigation. Condition dropdowns also replace full-editor and bulk-finish free text; unknown legacy assessments remain visible without silent upgrades.
- Raw singles priced at $20 or less take the PWE route. Over-$20 cards, slabs and parcel overrides use protective parcels; identified lots and comics never inherit PWE. Title lot/bundle cues are conservative, with an explicit lot option.
- $0.78 is the owner's reported label-cost baseline, not a live rate, buyer shipping charge, or purchased label. eBay's public standard-envelope page checked October 20 lists $0.82 at 1 oz. No rate is silently written into a seller policy.
- A uniquely identifiable existing envelope-only policy is preselected. Owner-measured PWE dimensions and confirmed packaging save once per seller and prefill later eligible groups. Changed packaging remains editable; eBay policy snapshots are refreshed and limits revalidated. Parcel/lot measurements are never reused from PWE.
- Lots/manual-review groups require measurements even with flat-rate policies, a positive current label-cost estimate, and confirmation that the buyer charge covers shipping and packaging. Core comic publication remains an explicit parity gap; this does not change the independent comic connector.
- Staged batches keep their identity when edited. An unapproved prepared batch is retained in history, cancelled locally and replaced automatically with a new group; old remote previews are not reused or approved. Final marketplace approval remains explicit. Approved/publishing/live/unknown batches are locked.
- Received-card price changes update the existing Vault record atomically without changing quantities, cost or original photos. CollX prices remain independent. Legacy fingerprints are unchanged until an optional shipping override is explicitly saved.
- Concurrent edits during shipping setup reject stale configuration. Auth, revision, idempotency, stale fingerprints, active preparation and stock locks remain enforced.

## Verification and release gate

- Unit/API regressions cover price thresholds, legacy fingerprints, conditions, atomic row edits, staged/remote batches, stock preservation, auth/replay, shipping cost review and concurrent configuration.
- Mobile simulated eBay flow covers inline editing without opening details, automatic price routing, prepared-preview replacement, reused PWE measurements, blank parcel measurements, reload, explicit final approval and verified synthetic readback. All existing seven browser suites remain required.
- Deploy only after independent-runtime (including disposable PostgreSQL, browser suites and Docker smoke), browser and vault checks pass at the exact candidate SHA. Verify Railway SUCCESS, exact commit, /healthz 0.6.4 and new UI assets. No real marketplace publication is part of release acceptance.

## Still not connected

CollX API posting, sales synchronization and automatic delisting; complete CDP parity; Core editing/ending of existing live marketplace listings; full Core comic listing lifecycle. Current CDP/CollX source exports and owner-approved real publication acceptance remain separate work.
