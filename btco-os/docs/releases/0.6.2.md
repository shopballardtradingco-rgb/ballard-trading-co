# BTCore 0.6.2 — Direct card listing flow

The previous UI forced the owner through separate price confirmation, listing-detail approval, saved-batch creation, shipping configuration and final review screens, with repeated navigation between them. A new List on eBay action takes a card or a selection through three visible steps: card details and editable asking prices, shipping, and final publication review.

Home, Intake, card review, individual cards and linked Vault records expose the direct action. Already approved cards and received cards with unconfirmed prices can be reviewed together. Continuing saves the owner-reviewed prices, card-detail approval and listing group in one revision-checked, idempotent operation. One invalid or stale row leaves all rows untouched. Original photos, exact SKUs, quantities and independent CollX prices are preserved. Saved groups block duplicate staging; sold/held/known-listed received inventory is rejected.

The selected group survives eBay connection and page reload. Preparation opens final review automatically. A sole available existing policy is preselected; multiple choices remain explicit. Final complete eBay review and owner approval remain mandatory; this release does not publish any real listing by itself.

Validation: unit/API regressions cover atomicity, stale revisions, invalid rows, authentication and operation replay. Mobile browser coverage starts with an approved draft plus a received card needing price confirmation, changes the received ask to $7, connects a simulated eBay seller, resumes into shipping, reaches final review, reloads without duplicate groups, and publishes only after the explicit final approval to simulated eBay. Exact-head independent-runtime (including PostgreSQL and Docker), browser and vault CI gate promotion.

No database schema change, new service or spending change. CollX direct posting, sales sync and automatic cross-channel delisting remain unconnected; see docs/CDP-REPLACEMENT.md. Real card publication still requires the owner to connect eBay and approve the exact final listings.
