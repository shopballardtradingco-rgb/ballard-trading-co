# BTCore 0.4.2 — price research and explicit asking prices

AI preparation now saves research separately and never fills or overwrites the asking price. New cards require an owner-entered ask. Existing stored prices and originals are preserved. Older AI-prepared cards without a separate price confirmation show Check price and cannot be approved, exported, staged or newly approved for eBay publication until the owner confirms an ask. Changing identity/parallel/grade invalidates price confirmation. Already-started publication recovery retains its exact previously approved revisions.

New numeric research suggestions require structured recent sale references: a URL returned by research, sale date within 180 days, visible actual USD sold amount, and matching subject, year, set, card number, parallel, print run, autograph status and grade. Hidden accepted offers, mismatches, missing dates, duplicate sources and checklist-only evidence are excluded. The suggestion is the median reported item price, rounded to a whole dollar, before shipping and fees. Unknown shipping remains unknown. These are AI-extracted references, not independently verified transactions; the UI says to check their links. No supported references means no numeric suggestion. The owner can always choose a manual ask.

Mobile Edit price / Check price is at the top of card details and beside card review prices. Saving unrelated details does not silently confirm a legacy price. New review is required after price changes. No live listings or production inventory values were modified as part of development.

Validation: full Core unit/API suite (PostgreSQL integration uses CI), mobile legacy-price regression, existing preparation/mobile flow, and simulated eBay publication. Release-branch CI now includes the standalone runtime, disposable PostgreSQL integration, mobile pricing and simulated eBay flows, and packaged Docker smoke check. No paid live AI or eBay publication is used in validation.

Deployment: Railway paused builds platform-wide on October 20, 2026. The existing development environment and branch remain the target. An hourly recovery watch is configured to verify the tested release and disable itself after successful deployment. Do not declare this release live from a configuration update alone: verify deployment commit and /healthz 0.4.2.
