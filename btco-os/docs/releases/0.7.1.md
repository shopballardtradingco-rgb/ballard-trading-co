# BTCore 0.7.1 — Use the identification and default conditions

Tim requested that identified card years never be re-entered unnecessarily, with raw eBay conditions defaulting to mint for Hoody’s cards or cards less than three years old, and good for the rest, subject to inline owner correction.

- Translate the requested raw-card labels to eBay’s supported vocabulary: Near mint or better and Very good. Based on the Pacific current calendar year, 2024–2026 qualify in 2026; 2023 does not. A recorded Hoody’s vendor/acquisition reference qualifies regardless of year. Never infer the vendor from a card title.
- Use the first year of a valid consecutive season (2001–02 → 2001; 1999–00 → 1999). For blank fields reuse an identified assessment year or an unambiguous year already in the title/set. Preserve nonempty unparseable manual text and conflicting years for correction rather than guessing. Keep the original season in metadata.
- Apply defaults to existing eligible unfinished/received cards through an authenticated, CSRF-protected, idempotent mutation with per-card content/source guards. Workspace GET stays read-only. Automatically save before rendering; unsaved inline edits are excluded. Future AI preparation applies the same rules. No database migration or remote listing write.
- Preserve explicit condition dropdown overrides, graded conditions, source notes, originals, master SKU and stock. Preserve a confirmed price when only an already-identified year is normalized. Retire obsolete unapproved previews and keep the replacement batch selected; never edit published/approved/unknown outcomes.
- Keep defaults visibly labeled and editable; they are owner-requested starting choices, not independent physical-condition findings. Owner confirmation is still required for exact publication.

Validation: focused domain/API cases for season normalization, ambiguous/missing years, age threshold, Hoody’s source, manual/graded overrides, stale source/content, publication/stock locks, received-record identity, confirmed-price continuity and idempotent replay; mobile eBay flow checks existing-card automatic defaults and manual override persistence. Require exact commit CI independent-runtime (including PostgreSQL/browser/Docker), browser and vault before promotion. Verify live 0.7.1 health and changed assets before saying deployed.

This includes 0.7.0’s one-page exceptions and single Publish confirmation. Cross-channel lifecycle syncing, automatic delisting and HC/Mercari/CollX connections are not enabled.
