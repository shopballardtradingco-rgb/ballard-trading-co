# BTCore 0.6.6 — Edit and publish from one screen

## Authorized scope

Tim requested bulk editing and bulk publishing from the same screen without repeatedly reviewing every card. This release changes the standalone Core development workflow. Standing authorization covers tested development deployment. Acceptance tests must not publish real marketplace listings.

## Behavior

- Intake and eBay listings share one checkbox selection, inline price/condition/shipping edits, an inline bulk editor, shared shipping settings, prepared listing summaries and Publish selected.
- Get ready to publish saves only selected pending edits and stages the ready selection. Incomplete rows remain visible with specific reasons; unrelated pending edits remain untouched.
- Existing unapproved batches resume or regroup automatically. Omitted cards keep a saved local batch, and old batch history is retained. An unchanged prepared selection reuses the exact existing review.
- Saved PWE choices apply across eligible singles. Entered parcel choices persist on this page only for the same shipping group members. Higher-priced cards, graded cards, lots and comics never inherit PWE measurements. Missing measurements or policies appear inline.
- After preparation, Publish selected is the explicit owner publication approval, bound to the exact saved review. Full originals, listing text, condition, prices and policies remain inspectable on this page. No extra confirmation checkbox or review modal is required.
- Changing the selection or selected card fields disables stale publish buttons. Edited unapproved previews refresh automatically; stock locks, stale revision checks, unknown-outcome protection and verified publication readback remain in force.
- Saved selection resumes after reload. Live links require verified publication results. No physical stock, original photos, master SKUs or independent CollX prices change during selection preparation.

## Verification and release gate

- Selection unit/API coverage: regrouping and omitted cards, unchanged review reuse, stale/duplicate/oversized/unconfirmed atomic rejection, publishing and stock locks, authentication, revision checks and idempotency.
- Synthetic mobile browser coverage: same selection for bulk edits and publication, selected-only saves, unrelated pending edits, incomplete-row exclusion, old saved batches, shipping reuse after edits, changed selection before publication, reload and verified links without duplicate publication.
- Bulk editor checked at 320×568, 390×664 and 844×390. Same-page publishing checked at 320 and 390 pixels wide. Browser tests use Chromium; native iPhone/Safari owner acceptance remains outstanding.
- Require all unit/API tests, eight browser suites, disposable PostgreSQL and Docker smoke, plus exact-candidate independent-runtime, browser and vault CI success. Verify Railway SUCCESS for that SHA, /healthz 0.6.6 and matching app, policy and style assets.

## Remaining limits

Comics marketplace publication, live listing edit/end controls, CollX direct posting/API sales sync and automatic cross-channel delisting remain separate gaps. This release does not complete CDP parity or establish that the owner's real cards have been published.
