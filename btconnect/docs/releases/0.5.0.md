# 0.5.0 candidate — hosted comic assembly and resumable preparation

Build impact: BTConnect only. No deployment or production mutation in this task.

The caller previously authored every complete nested draft and preparation
rechecked successful books on resume. `prepare_comic_intake` now constructs drafts
on the existing service from per-comic evidence and one set of shared settings.
It produces stable IDs/SKUs, mandatory disclosures, explicit offer terms and
individualized text; incomplete entries stay excluded. Pricing evidence is saved
with the exact review rather than inserted into buyer descriptions.

After explicit key creation and $5 test-budget authorization, optional hosted
original-photo analysis and web-sourced pricing proposals were added. Results are
saved with immutable request IDs and exact revisions. The owner must review the
research before it can become a draft; final publication approval remains separate.
The existing encrypted store also enforces the nonrenewing pilot budget. Missing
usage and interrupted requests retain conservative reservations. No card publication
or independently authenticated sold-data feed is introduced.

Preparation is bounded to five attempts / 35 seconds. Pending work precedes failed
entries and unchanged successes are retained. Timings and attempts survive restart.
The original publisher still performs fresh photo, policy, duplicate and content
checks before writes. Existing exact-revision approval and uncertain-outcome
reconciliation remain mandatory.
