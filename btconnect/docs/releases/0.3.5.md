# Connector 0.3.5 — exterior-only comic inspection

BTCo does not inspect comic interiors and discloses that limitation to buyers. Interior inspection is not a prerequisite for preparing a listing. This release separates identification supported by exterior photographs and catalog references from unverified printing, variant, interior condition and completeness.

## Listing contract

- `inspection` records `scope: "exterior_only"` or `"interior_inspected"` plus actual evidence; no inspection is silently inferred.
- `identity.verification_scope: "cover_identity"` verifies only series, issue, year and publisher against evidence. `printing` and `variant` must both be null, and their item specifics are omitted. The existing `full_identity` path continues to require known printing and variant. Neither path verifies interior condition by itself.
- Exterior-only drafts require this sentence in both the main description and condition description: **Interior pages have not been inspected; interior condition and completeness are unverified.**
- Exterior-only drafts use `condition: null`, preserving descriptive exterior wear without assigning a grade that implies verified pages. Publication checks current category metadata and proceeds only when `itemConditionRequired` is explicitly boolean false. Missing, ambiguous or true metadata blocks the write. No fallback condition is invented if eBay rejects an omitted value.
- Photos, text, identity, inspection scope/evidence, shipping and offer settings all participate in the exact revision hash. Content changes clear approval. Legacy drafts lacking explicit inspection evidence must be re-saved and approved; the release does not migrate or grant approval.

Free-text evidence and descriptions still require review. The schema does not purport to prove every natural-language claim. Do not add first-printing, intact-pages, completeness, restoration-free or interior-grade claims without evidence.

## Existing automatic promotion

`promotion_mode: "automatic_existing"` supports a reviewed, already running US fixed cost-per-sale campaign at exactly 5%, with automatic future inventory selection and a marketplace-wide rule. The campaign snapshot is approval-bound. The connector does not create a campaign, change a rate or budget, alter a selection rule, or manually create an ad in this mode.

After publication, it checks the selected campaign for the actual listing ID and exact 5% ad rate. If eBay has not yet included the listing, publication remains durably recorded and retries only read it back. A verified receipt is withheld until the ad is verified; no second offer or duplicate listing is created. Existing manual campaigns retain explicit per-ad 5% behavior.

## Verification and limits

213 tests pass using synthetic photographs and mocked eBay calls. New tests cover exterior-only publication and retries, mandatory disclosures, unknown edition preservation, category permission, unexpected grades on read-back, approval invalidation, automatic campaign rates and recovery without ad writes. The suite does not assert a real eBay publication succeeded.

No existing inventory, CDP SKU, business policy, campaign or permission is changed by this deployment. The pilot remains unpublished pending approval of its complete saved disclosure revision and successful eBay verification. Shipping estimates and whole-dollar Best Offer terms remain as previously implemented.
