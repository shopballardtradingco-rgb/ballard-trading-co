## First owner-started batch

   five new comics. JPEG or single-frame PNG; at most 12 MB per file. Native
   transfer accepts 10 files per call; preserve successful transfers and retry
   only failures. Use file_index, not guessed host file IDs.
2. Compile one saved batch. Use evidence for identity and condition, whole-dollar
   prices, offers off at $5 and the reviewed offer floor otherwise. Include the
   exterior-only inspection disclosures when interiors were not checked.
3. Bind packing to the actual item and method. A Gemini single-comic estimate
   does not apply to the cardboard/poly method, lots, slabs or larger books.
   Use measured values or an applicable owner-authorized estimate.
4. Show the complete review and isolate exceptions. New exact review approval
   precedes publication. Verify live ID, photos, price, condition, shipping and
   promotion; do not replay old drafts or uncertain outcomes as test listings.
5. Reopen/resume the saved batch without re-uploading or recreating successes.
   Then continue in manageable batches, normally 20 items per review. Transfer
   and server preparation chunks are not separate owner approval requests.
